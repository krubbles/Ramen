This document describes the rules of Balatro.

# High Level Overview
- A game of Balatro consists of 24 rounds. 
- The player proceeds through the rounds in order. 
- During each round, the player starts with 0 points. Then, they may play up to a limited number of hands. Playing a hand scores points, increasing their running total for the round. 
- Each round has a threshold score. When the players total round score exceeds the threshold, they win the round. 
- If the player runs out of hands before exceeding the threshold score, they lose the game.
